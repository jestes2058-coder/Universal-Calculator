Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026 | Last Updated: November 3, 2026 | Version: 2.1

1. Executive Summary & Privacy-First Commitment

Universal Calculator ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This website is built on a privacy-by-design architecture. Unlike standard modern websites, we deliberately operate without third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, behavioral tracking pixels, or third-party analytical scripts (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel).

This policy explains how we process information in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU & UK GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), and global privacy frameworks.

2. Data Controller Information

The data controller responsible for the processing of your personal data on this website is:

Universal Calculator Engineering Tools

Operator: Universal Calculator Technical Operations

Designated Privacy Officer Contact: privacy@universalcalculator-sample.local

Jurisdiction: Standard Global Engineering Reference Service

3. Information We Collect (Data Minimization)

In adherence to the principle of Data Minimization (GDPR Article 5(1)(c)), we only collect what is strictly necessary:

  • Calculator Operations (Zero Personal Data): All resistance calculations (2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 band calculations and reverse calculations) are executed 100% on your local device (client-side JavaScript). Your color band choices and inputs are never sent to, logged by, or stored on our servers.
  • Local Storage Settings: We store minimal functional preferences in your web browser’s local storage (e.g., your preference to dismiss the cookie notice). This data remains exclusively on your device.
  • Contact Form Communications (Voluntary): If you deliberately choose to submit an inquiry through our Contact Form, we collect your Name, Email Address, Subject, and Message content. This information is processed exclusively to address your inquiry.
  • Server Logs (Standard Technical Operation): Like all web servers, standard non-identifying server access logs (HTTP request timestamp, response status code, requested URL) may be momentarily processed for routing and anti-DDoS security. We do not aggregate these logs into individual user profiles.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We process personal data only when an established lawful basis applies:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): When you voluntarily submit a contact form message and check the explicit consent box. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Maintaining server stability, mitigating bot abuse, and ensuring cybersecurity integrity.

5. No Sale or Sharing of Personal Data (CCPA/CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • We do not sell personal information to third parties.
  • We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We have not sold or shared any consumer personal information in the preceding 12 months.

6. Third-Party Embeds and Font Policies

To maintain airtight data privacy and prevent illegal IP transmission under European privacy jurisprudence (such as the Munich Regional Court Google Fonts ruling):

  • We do not load external fonts from Google Fonts or Adobe Typekit. We use standard system UI fonts available natively on your operating system.
  • We do not embed third-party YouTube, Vimeo, or social tracking widgets.
  • All icons and resistor diagrams are 100% custom inline SVGs hosted directly in the HTML document.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Inquiries submitted via our contact form are retained only for as long as necessary to answer your request (typically up to 60 calendar days), after which they are permanently purged from our support inbox.

8. Your Rights Under International Privacy Law

Regardless of your geographic location, you enjoy the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your contact data.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection: Object to processing under legitimate interest.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (such as the ICO in the UK, CNIL in France, or your state Attorney General).

To exercise any of these rights, email us directly at privacy@universalcalculator-sample.local.

9. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Our services are educational in nature and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you suspect a minor has submitted personal contact details, please notify us immediately for prompt deletion.

10. Updates to This Policy

Any modifications to this Privacy Policy will be posted directly to this URL with an updated effective date. Continued use of the website following changes constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
