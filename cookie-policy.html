Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026 | Last Audited: November 3, 2026

1. Overview: Zero Advertising Trackers

Unlike modern commercial websites filled with marketing cookies, behavioral profilers, and advertising beacons, Universal Calculator operates on a strict zero-surveillance model.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics pixels (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel), or user-fingerprinting techniques.

2. Do We Need Cookie Consent? (Legal Analysis)

Under the EU ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC, Art. 5(3)), the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), and California privacy statutes:

  • Strictly Necessary Storage: Cookies or local storage keys that are strictly required to deliver a feature specifically requested by the visitor (such as remembering calculator states or acknowledging a privacy banner) are exempt from prior opt-in consent requirements.
  • Our Approach: Because our website uses only strictly necessary functional storage and zero tracking/marketing technologies, prior opt-in consent gates are not legally required under EU law. However, in the interest of maximum transparency and complete protection against regulatory ambiguity, we provide a clear informational banner and this exhaustive storage audit.

4. Third-Party Cookies: None

This website does not set or permit any third-party cookies. When you visit this site, no cookies are transmitted to advertising brokers, data aggregators, or external cloud infrastructure.

5. How to Control and Clear Browser Storage

You have full control over your browser's stored data. You can clear or block cookies and local storage at any time via your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data → See all site data and permissions.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.

Note: Clearing local storage simply resets the calculator to its default 4-band state and redisplays the informational notice.
