Because standard access to this website requires zero financial consideration, monetary refunds are generally non-applicable to public web visitors. No user can be charged without their knowledge, as this website contains no payment gateways, automatic billing mechanisms, or paywalls.

Any directly purchased digital resource or service carries a 30-day satisfaction guarantee from the purchase date. If you encounter defects or discrepancies from stated engineering standards, you are entitled to a full refund.

2.2 Statutory Right of Withdrawal (EU / UK Consumers)

Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive (Directive 2011/83/EU) and the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations, European and British consumers possess a statutory right to cancel a digital purchase within 14 calendar days without stating grounds, unless immediate performance was explicitly consented to with acknowledgment of loss of withdrawal rights upon download.

2.3 Refund Request Process

To request a refund under an eligible commercial purchase:

  • Send an email to support@universalcalculator-sample.local with the subject line Refund Request - [Order ID].
  • Include the original transaction receipt and the reason for your refund request.
  • Our billing team will review and acknowledge your request within 2 business days.

2.4 Method & Timelines of Reimbursement

Approved refunds will be processed via the original payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal, or wire transfer) within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your financial institution's processing cycles.

3. Fraudulent Charges & Dispute Prevention

We take consumer security seriously. We encourage all users to contact our support team directly at support@universalcalculator-sample.local before initiating a formal bank chargeback, as we resolve genuine transaction errors promptly and cooperatively.
